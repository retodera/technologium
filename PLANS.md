## Blocks
### Drone Station
- create drones
- drone will be around the station when idle
- if a request block needs items, the closest idling drone will be sent towards the closest supply block and deliver the items
- drones aren't binded to the station, they will fly to the closest one once idle
- just a unit factory, most of the work here is the AI of the drones
- will have four types of drones: standart, more capacious, faster, armored
### Manipulator
- would work exactly like one from factorio
### Interplanetary Launch Pad
- launch pad but send to other planets, or perhaps moons/satellites
### Icy Floor
- for future Itinbu campaign
- when units walk/drive over, they slide as if they're on ice (they will)
### Deconstructable Floor
- for future Nobata campaign
- would function exactly as told in the name
### Lifter
- for future Nobata campaign
- could be built only on top of liquids, will create lifting force around itself in a radius
- could be boosted by inputting fuel, will make more lifting force

- would require pressure to not be damaged
### Liquid Unloader
- works exactly as an unloader but for liquids
### Logic Extender

### Module Applyer
- applies modules to units (well that was obvious)
### Bioreactor
- neoplasm, water, some hot liquid on input
- lots of power on output
- if no hot liquid, enemy neoplastic units will spawn and destroy everything
### Biofirmware inserter
- neoplasm, water, bioprocessors, power on input
