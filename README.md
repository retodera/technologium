## A bit about the mod
The mod took a bit of inspiration from Factorio - that's why the mod has "technologies" - complex items that are required to construct blocks, units or other complex items.

The Kudol idea was taken from the Erekir planet. It'll be like a tutorial to the mod itself. After completion, access to Venjer and Nobata will be open.  
Venjer was randomly made up and chosen as the hardest planet. It's campaign will be similar to Gleba from Factorio Space Age.   
Nobata will be the most interesting planet, because there will be no ores nor items, thus [liquids would be required to build](PLANS.md#build-out-of-liquids), and will have a brand new [Lifting mechanic](PLANS.md#lifting-force).  
Itinbu would have a pretty short campaign, with small maps.
